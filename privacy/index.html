Privacy policy

This page explains what happens to your details when you sign up on byleo.org.

What we collect

Only your email address, when you enter it in a sign-up form.

Why

To send you what you asked for (for example, the free Yoga Nidra audio) and, now and then, short emails with practical tips for calm, focus and better rest.

Who processes it

Emails are stored and sent with systeme.io, our email and course platform. We don't sell or share your email with anyone else.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also ask us to delete your details at any time — just write to leo@byleo.org.
